# Cancellation policy

Category: Billing
Effective: 2026-10-01 unless another date is explicitly stated below.
Fictional Cedarline Cloud documentation.

## Policy and procedure

The workspace owner can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Paid access continues until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund. After paid access ends, the workspace enters read-only mode for 30 days before deletion.
