# Troubleshoot E-310

Category: Troubleshooting
Effective: 2026-10-01 unless another date is explicitly stated below.
Fictional Cedarline Cloud documentation.

## Policy and procedure

Error E-310 means the workspace storage cap has been reached. The owner or an admin can remove unnecessary files, or the owner can upgrade the plan. Existing files remain readable and downloadable. Support cannot remove private files on your behalf.
