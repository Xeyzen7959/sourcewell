# Plans and seats

Category: Billing
Effective: 2026-10-01 unless another date is explicitly stated below.
Fictional Cedarline Cloud documentation.

## Policy and procedure

Starter costs USD 12 per active member per month. Studio costs USD 24 per active member per month. Prices exclude taxes. Both plans are billed monthly. Annual billing is not currently offered. A suspended member does not consume a paid seat.
