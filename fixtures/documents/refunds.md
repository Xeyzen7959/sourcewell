# Refund policy

Category: Billing
Effective: 2026-10-01 unless another date is explicitly stated below.
Fictional Cedarline Cloud documentation.

## Policy and procedure

A first-time workspace subscription is eligible for a full refund if requested within 14 calendar days of the initial payment. Renewals and prorated seat charges are non-refundable. To request an eligible refund, contact support with the workspace ID and invoice number. Approval and processing are handled by a human specialist.
