# Reset your password

Category: Account
Effective: 2026-10-01 unless another date is explicitly stated below.
Fictional Cedarline Cloud documentation.

## Policy and procedure

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen and enter your account email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If the message does not arrive within 10 minutes, check spam and confirm the email address. Support will never ask for your password.
